# Asset notes

The wedding, family, church, counselling, couple and fictional profile images were created using the built-in OpenAI image generation tool for this showcase. They do not depict clients, verified members, staff or success stories. The contact sheets were extracted into their original individual frames and encoded as WebP for the website. No source Imperial Matrimonial photography was reused.

## Prompts

Hero: Premium natural editorial Indian Christian wedding photography. South Indian bride in an ivory gown and veil with a white rose bouquet; groom in a warm ivory suit. A candid affectionate moment in a lush garden courtyard of a South Indian church. Muted forest foliage, champagne warmth, morning sun, pale stone arches, believable skin texture and gentle film grain. No text, logos, watermark or exaggerated lighting.

Story images: Four equal photographs with consistent natural editorial light. An Indian couple with their parents in an airy ivory living room; a quiet South Indian church interior with pale arches and wooden pews; a pastor and couple in a calm counselling room; a couple walking in lush greenery at golden hour. Warm ivory, forest green and champagne tones. No signage, visible text or overlays.

Portraits: Four equal independent natural portraits of fictional South Indian adults. A 31-year-old man in a cream linen shirt; a 30-year-old man with glasses in a forest-green shirt; a 32-year-old man in a sage shirt; a 28-year-old woman in an ivory blouse. Soft green garden backgrounds, natural expressions and skin texture, premium 85mm editorial style. No captions, watermark or logos.
